Theo

A quiet daily record for your dog or cat — meals, walks, medication, mood, and weight, plus the vet details you need at the worst possible moment.

Support

Something broken, confusing, or missing? Email support@theosdogapp.com and describe what you were doing when it happened. Theo has no analytics and no crash reporting, so a message from you is genuinely the only way a problem gets found.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?
On your device, and mirrored to your own private iCloud account. There is no Theo server, and the developer cannot read your records. See the privacy policy.
My records aren't appearing on my other device.
Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive enabled. Sync runs in the background and can take a moment after a change.
Why does Theo ask for my location?
Only to find dog parks near you, and only at the moment you search. The location is used for that one search and discarded — it is never stored or transmitted to the developer.
How do I delete everything?
Settings → Delete Account. This removes Theo's data from your device and deletes its record zone from your iCloud account.
Does Theo work without iCloud?
Yes. Everything works locally. Without iCloud your records simply stay on that one device and are not backed up by sync.
